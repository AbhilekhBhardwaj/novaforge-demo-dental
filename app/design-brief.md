# Brightwell Family Dental - design brief

Design read: families in Cary, especially people who dread the dentist; register is calm, sunlit, reassuring, never clinical.
Concept spine: "a bright well" - light pooling into a quiet room. Water-light caustics recur (hero film, section plates, soft ripple motifs).
Delivery tier: editorial with cinematic hero (user-specified autoplay loop hero + layered scroll parallax).
Animation mode: non-animated - user explicitly specified an autoplaying, muted, looped hero video with 2-3 depth scroll parallax (no scroll-scrub).
Locked palette: lagoon ink #0E3A40, deep teal #1D5A60, sea-glass #CFE6DF, rinse paper #F4F8F5, honey sun #F2B84B. Defense: fresh water + morning light; teal reads clean without hospital blue, honey adds warmth for anxious visitors.
Locked type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, friendly soft grotesque with optical sizes) + Figtree (body, round and legible).
Section plan: hero (full-bleed film, left-weighted copy) / about (asymmetric split, image plate with inner parallax) / services (4-up icon grid, emergency card inverted) / reviews (full-bleed plate + staggered quote cards) / visit (hours + live open state, location + generic Cary map) / footer.
Asset plan (Higgsfield, credit cap 225): hero still gpt_image_2_5 (2.75) -> Kling 3.0 pro 10s silent loop start=end frame; about room still; windowsill still; botanical cutout foreground layer; hand-drawn SVG logo, favicon, service icons; OG composed in-browser from hero still.
CTA inventory: header call pill (fills with honey on hover); hero primary tap-to-call slab (arrow slides); hero ghost link (underline grows); emergency card call link; mobile floating call bubble; footer phone.
